Catering questions

Do you bring the taco truck to our event?
Yes. We park the Brothers Taquizas truck at your event and cook tacos fresh on site, so guests get them hot off the grill. If you'd rather pick food up, we also pack catering trays at 3707 Broadway in Riviera Beach.
What areas do you cater?
Palm Beach County and nearby South Florida, including Riviera Beach, West Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, Wellington and Lake Worth. Send us your event location and we'll confirm.
What do you need from us on site?
A level spot where we can park the trailer near your guests. We'll go over space and any power needs with you when we send your quote.
How far ahead should we book?
As early as you can, since weekend dates go first. For something short notice, call 561-201-5724 and we'll tell you right away if we can make it work.
Can you handle dietary needs?
Yes. Tell us about vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free guests in your quote request and we'll build options into your menu.
